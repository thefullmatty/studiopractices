Studio Recipes

A recipe offers ingredients, steps, and conditions, but the making is yours.

Use this tool to compose a studio recipe from your site of inquiry. Select or write an action, choose how you will document it, and add a journal prompt to help you reflect on what happens.

The recipe builder will help you turn these parts into an editable studio recipe. You can revise it, make it more specific to your project, then copy or download it as a prompt for your next studio session.

You can follow the recipe closely, adapt it, or use it as a starting point for something unexpected.

Some recipes work. Some fail. Both can teach you something.

A recipe is not a rule. It is a way to begin.
